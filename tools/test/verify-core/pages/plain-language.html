Public Assistance Program Guidelines

Administrative Provisions and Appeals

On November 15, 2026, the Department of Public Welfare established that eligible beneficiaries may appeal determinations within 45 calendar days by submitting Form 1040-A to the Central Review Office or through the official online claims portal.

Notwithstanding prior stipulations, participants receiving supplemental nutritional allowances under subsection 12 must report any variance in aggregate household revenue exceeding $250 within ten business days to maintain continuous eligibility under state guidelines.

Please call our support center if you need help with your application.
