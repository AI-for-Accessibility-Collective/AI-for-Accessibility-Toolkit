Accessibility Guidelines and Standards

Motion in interface elements can cause severe physical reactions including vertigo and seizures. Following WCAG 2.2 Success Criterion 2.2.2 (Pause, Stop, Hide) and 2.3.1 (Three Flashes or Below Threshold), our platform respects user autonomy and provides steady, predictable presentations.
