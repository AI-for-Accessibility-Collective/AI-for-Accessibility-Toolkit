Accessible Typography in Digital Publishing

Clear typography improves comprehension and reduces visual fatigue for readers of all abilities. When text can be enlarged and given generous line and letter spacing, readers with low vision or reading challenges can read comfortably without losing their place.

Captions and auxiliary notes are frequently styled with explicit pixel font sizes in production layouts.
