Planting a winter garden

By Sam Rivera · 3 November 2026 · 6 minute read

Winter is a good time to plan next year's beds. The soil is resting, and you can see the shape of the garden without leaves in the way.

Choose hardy plants

Kale, leeks and winter onions keep growing through cold weather. Plant them before the first hard frost so their roots settle.

Protect the soil

Cover empty beds with leaves or straw. It keeps the soil from washing away and feeds it as it breaks down.
