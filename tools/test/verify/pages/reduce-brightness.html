Sensory Comfort

Creating a Calmer Visual Workspace

Published on November 3, 2026 • By Dr. Alex Rivera

High screen brightness and harsh contrast can trigger visual fatigue, migraines, and sensory overload. Subtle reductions in overall luminance help relax ocular tension without sacrificing readability.

Unlike dark mode inversion, which shifts black and white polarity, brightness attenuation keeps typography, diagrams, and layouts familiar and steady.
