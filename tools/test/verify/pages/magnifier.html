Cultivating Winter Orchids Indoors

Published on November 3, 2026 by Dr. Elena Vance

Orchids require delicate humidity balance, indirect light, and well-draining substrates during dormancy.

Notice: Specimen humidity levels must strictly remain between 55% and 65% RH. Avoid direct midday southern sun exposure to prevent irreversible leaf necrosis and pseudobulb dehydration.

When repotting epiphytic orchids after bloom senescence, inspect the root system thoroughly for fungal rot or dehydration. Healthy roots appear plump and silvery-green when dry, turning emerald green upon hydration. Trim brittle brown roots using sterilised shears dipped in 70% isopropyl alcohol, then pack the root ball into fresh New Zealand sphagnum moss or coarse fir bark. Maintain ambient room temperatures around twenty-one degrees Celsius throughout the vegetative growth period to stimulate strong spike formation.

Dormancy Season Reminder

Watering intervals should decrease by half from late November through early February.
