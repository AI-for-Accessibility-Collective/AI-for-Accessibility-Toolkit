Ensuring Content Reflows Without Pinned Chrome Barriers

When users with low vision zoom in to 400%, sticky and fixed headers consume valuable viewport space. WCAG 2.2 Success Criterion 1.4.10 (Reflow) requires that content can be presented without loss of information or functionality, and without requiring scrolling in two dimensions.

1. Understanding Reflow

Fixed position elements remain anchored to the viewport regardless of scrolling. At high zoom levels, a 60-pixel header combined with a floating cookie banner and sticky navigation can obscure more than half of the visible screen area, leaving only a tiny horizontal slit for reading body text.

Additional analysis shows that unpinning these bars allows them to stay in the natural document flow, scrolling out of view with the rest of the page.

2. WCAG 2.2 Guidelines

In addition to reflow, Success Criterion 2.4.11 (Focus Not Obscured) ensures that when an interactive component receives keyboard focus, it is not entirely covered by author-created content such as pinned banners.
