Understanding Quantum Superposition

Quantum superposition is a fundamental principle of quantum mechanics holding that physical systems may exist in multiple states simultaneously.

Unlike classical computers which rely on binary bits, quantum computers manipulate qubits that can represent zero and one at the same time.
