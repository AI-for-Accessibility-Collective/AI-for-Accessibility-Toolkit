Sensory-Friendly Multimedia on the Modern Web

Unexpected sound from autoplaying video and background audio can cause acute disorientation and anxiety, particularly for neurodivergent and sensory-sensitive people. Providing reliable controls to keep browsing calm and quiet makes web experiences fundamentally usable.
