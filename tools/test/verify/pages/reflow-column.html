Lead Story: The Future of Single-Column Reading

Multi-column layouts create barriers for people who zoom in to 400% or use high-magnification displays. When a page relies on side-by-side floated containers, text columns are compressed into narrow strips forcing readers into difficult two-dimensional scrolling.

Modern web accessibility guidelines require pages to reflow content without requiring scrolling in two dimensions. Column count properties divide paragraphs into narrow ribbons that are hard to track.

When single-column reflow is engaged, these artificial columns collapse into one flowing column of text that honors the natural reading order and natural measure.

Readers can comfortably follow the narrative without jumping back and forth across wide viewport chasms or untangling intersecting columns of content.
