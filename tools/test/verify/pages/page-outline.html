Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for financial assistance under the community support programme, applicants must reside within the designated administrative district and demonstrate hardship.

  • Over eighteen years of age
  • Current municipal resident for at least six months
  • Household income below local reference threshold

Required Documentation

Before beginning your submission, collect the following verification materials to prevent delays.

Proof of Identity

A valid passport, national identity card, or driving license issued by the relevant authority.

Proof of Address and Income

Recent utility bills, rental agreements, or tenancy agreements issued within the past three months.

Accepted Document Formats

Files must be provided in PDF or PNG format, under ten megabytes per document.

How to Apply

Applications can be submitted via the digital portal or in person at registered civic advice centres.

  1. Create or log in to your civic account.
  2. Fill out personal and household information.
  3. Attach supporting digital documentation.
  4. Review your summary and submit.
Appeals and Reviews

If an initial determination is contested, applicants have thirty calendar days from notification to request an independent supervisory review.
