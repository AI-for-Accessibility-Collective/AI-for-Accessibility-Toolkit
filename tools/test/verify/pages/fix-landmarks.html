Improving Accessibility with Semantic Landmarks

ARIA landmarks enable assistive technology users to quickly orient themselves and navigate to major sections of a page. Screen readers provide keyboard shortcuts to jump directly to the banner, navigation, main content, and contentinfo regions. Without explicit HTML5 semantic tags or ARIA landmark roles, users may have to traverse dozens of repetitive elements to locate the primary information.

Landmark Navigation: Blind screen reader users frequently use landmark navigation as their first step when exploring a web page.

Why Top-Level Roles Matter

When building or retrofitting web applications, landmarks should sit at the top level of the document hierarchy rather than nesting inappropriately. A banner represents the primary site header, while contentinfo represents the footer. Keeping landmarks properly defined improves screen-reader predictability.

For more details on landmark navigation, refer to the W3C ARIA Authoring Practices Guide and our recent summary article for helpful implementation tips and background.
