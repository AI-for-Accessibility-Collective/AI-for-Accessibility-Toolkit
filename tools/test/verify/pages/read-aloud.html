The Search for Water on Mars

Recent rover discoveries indicate ancient lake beds on Mars with geological features shaped by flowing water billions of years ago.

Scientists have identified sedimentary rock layers containing clay minerals. These formations preserve chemical signatures that suggest standing liquid water persisted for millions of years.

"Liquid water was once abundant enough to sculpt vast canyons and nourish possible microbial habitats."

Key Discoveries

  • Sedimentary delta deposits in Jezero crater.
  • Subsurface water ice reservoirs detected by orbiting radars.
  • Seasonal dark streaks possibly caused by hydrated salts.
